					I suppose that the same variety of understanding in the minds of the people pertaining to the kingdom the same as it is on other subjects [space] we frequently here the elders refer to the kingdom of God to this people possessing it as having the ordinances of the house of God or the kingdom of God [space] I wish to inform all the elders of Israel [--?] with[?] their wives and their children and then all those that stand and look on those whose eyes are upon this kingdom or upon the results of the kingdom in the midst of the people [space] that when the kingdom of God is established upon the earth that each individual singly do his or her duty the kingdom of God always takes care of itself] it is a living moving thing [space] the priesthood of the Son of God comprises in its operations the kingdom of God if we were to ask what the priesthood of the Son of God is I know of no better language used what has been conveyed to me by the Spirit that is to say to the people it is a pure system of government] those let[?] those that are governed live according to this system of laws of this pure government don't you see that all harmonizes and each and every part of this kingdom or the members of the kingdom remains one with the other and he moves right along without any difficulty this is the fact with regard to what we see this great truth Brother Spencer referred [crossed out: to greater] this is a great truth a great fact that now exists and is before the eyes of the nations of the earth kings and rulers princes potentates presidents magistrates governor and officers with all the people in masses is a living living [sic] thing [space] besides this that we see to the acts of Joseph Smith [space] to any other man that ever lived or ever will to attribute any great power government power to any individual is a wrong conclusion in the minds of each and every individual that come to this conclusion it is not one man two a score a 100 nor a thousand that carries on this work the great architect and manager the superintendent the controller dictator the one that guides is out of sight of our natural eyes] he lives on another planet, [crossed out: he] is in another state of existence, he has passed the ordeals we are passing through he has received an experience, suffered enjoyed and knows all that you and I can know and just as we are passing through the whole of it he is crowned exalted and he holds the keys of power of this kingdom and sways his scepter to do his pleasure and does his will among the children of men saint or sinner and brings forth the results of kingdoms and kings of nations emperor and all redound to his glory and to the perfection of his work [space] it is not any man that sits[?] upon this earth that carries on this work it guides itself it directs itself by the great machinist whose work and workmanship we are whose kingdom this is it is all governed and controlled by him who knows all things] and he will bring forth the and he will bring forth the righteous and the just [inserted above line: and] humble [inserted above line: the meek of the earth all] those that love to serve him and keep his commandments and enjoy the fullness of his glory [space] these ordinances this kingdom and work is proffered to the whole human family all who desire may accept all the kingdom upon the terms of strict obedience not matter whether they are prophets apostles whether they have the gift of seeing[?] of faith whether they have the gift of tongues or an interpretation of tongues those power or that power no matter what it is they receive the kingdom the glory and the benefits of this kingdom upon the simple[?] terms of strict obedience to the laws and requirements of it and when all every body the kingdom moves along the old ship Zion won't stop and you and I may be satisfied we see the hopes and the providences of men we see them run to the west to the east to the north to the south and here and there [space] those that have received the gospel and rejoice in it and yet the trials of the world they are thrown upon the ocean with their little bark without compasses rudder pilot anything to dictate and they are like the fowls aye/eye[?] to the ends of the have no wind whither to go to seek for that joy and peace their heart seeks for this is what brings on that confusion and a destruction the people that understand this and the cause of men's and women's becoming confused with regard to this kingdom here it is a living miracle to all its beholders
