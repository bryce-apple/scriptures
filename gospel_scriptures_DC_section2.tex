	% -----
	\subsubsection{Historical Background}
	% -----
		\label{DC2-HB}
		
		Section 2 is an extract from JS's personal history, and was composed in 1838. There is no record contemporaneous to this event which states what Moroni said, or how he quoted these scriptures. 
		
		Here is a more complete extract from \href{https://www.josephsmithpapers.org/paper-summary/history-1838-1856-volume-a-1-23-december-1805-30-august-1834/5}{volume 1-A} of the history:
		
			\begin{quote}
				After telling me these things he commenced quoting the prophecies of the Old testament, he first quoted part of the third chapter of Malachi and he quoted also the fourth or last chapter of the same prophecy though with a little variation from the way it reads in our Bibles. Instead of quoting the first verse as reads in our books he quoted it thus, ``For behold the day cometh that shall burn as an oven, and all the proud <yea> and all that do wickedly shall burn as stubble, for <the\sout{y} day> that cometh shall burn them saith the Lord of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch.'' And again he quoted the fifth verse thus, ``Behold I will reveal unto you the Priesthood by the hand of Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord.'' He also quoted the next verse differently, ``And he shall plant in the hearts of the Children the promises made to the fathers, and the hearts of the children shall turn to their fathers, if it were not so the whole earth would be utterly wasted at his coming.''
			\end{quote}
		
		% --
		\paragraph{HDDC}
		% --
		
			``Section 2 was not published as part of the D\&C until 1876 when Elder Orson Pratt, under the direction of President Brigham Young, included it in that edition...
			
			``Since Moroni's visit to Joseph Smith is part of the Prophet's history, Section 2 was widely published in early periodicals of the Church...the earliest one is found in the Manuscript History of the Church, which is the complete hand-written history of the Prophet'' (126--127).

	\subsubsection{Section 2:1} % *DC2-1 
	% -----
		\label{DC2-1}

			\footnote{%
				% \label{}%
				See \hyperref[EXSS-OT-MALACHI-4-VERSIONS]{this study} for comparisons between these verses and other accounts of the \hyperref[MALACHI-4]{Malachi 4} verses. 
				}%
		BEHOLD, I will reveal unto you the Priesthood, by the hand of Elijah the prophet, before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord.%
			\footnote{%
				% \label{}%
				The key addition to \hyperref[MALACHI-4-5]{Malachi 4:5} is the bit about ``revealing the Priesthood'' by Elijah's hand. This ``prophecy'' appears to have been fulfilled with the visitation recorded in \hyperref[DC110]{DC 110}, but that event took place in April 1836, while this part of the DC was actually composed in 1838. So it seems more like a retroactive addition to what Moroni said at the time, which might be similar to some of the ways that the first vision accounts developed.
				}
