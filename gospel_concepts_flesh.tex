				\item[Commentary] I'm wondering if this isn't basically the same as \textit{temporal} in the BOM; I won't go into it now, but check out 1N 22:1--6 for example. See also \hyperref[LCHRIST-036-J]{John 3:4--6} for a distinction between flesh and spirit.
				\item Based on passages like \hyperref[ALMA-7-12]{Alma 7:12}, as well, I think ``according to the flesh'' may sometimes have the meaning, ``by means of/because of/on the basis of experiences had with a physical body.''
