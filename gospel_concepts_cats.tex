	\subsubsection{Brigham Young} % *BY
	% -----
		\label{CATS-BY}
	
		\begin{compactdesc} % Format is: 21 May 1843 HC-a, or whatever date, whatever record-keeper (if there are multiple), then the passage
			\item[19 June 1853] I will now say, not only to our delegate to congress, but to the Elders that leave the body of the church. He (our delegate) thought that all the cats, and kittens were let out of the bag\sout{g}, when Brother Pratt went back last fall and published the Revelation conserning \sout{A}the \sout{Marr} plurality of wives, it was thought there was no other cat to let out,. \sout{b}But alow me to tell you Elders of Israel, \sout{and} and delegates to Congress; You may expect an eternity of cats, that have not yet escaped from the bag\sout{g}. Bless your souls there is no end \sout{t}of them. For if there is not one thing, there will always be another.
				\begin{compactitem}
					\item I transcribed this quotation myself, on 3/7/2024, from the longhand report. It's CR 100 317 b2 f11, pages 6--7, PDF pages 7--8. View it \href{https://catalog.churchofjesuschrist.org/assets/d01ece03-48fe-4555-b135-6f7e82112133/0/0}{here} for the CHL, or \href{https://drive.google.com/file/d/1r3YMPdAcCJnLoR-ys1udo4_W-AyJIpH0/view?usp=drive_link}{here} for my Google drive. There is shorthand for this address, and I've asked LaJean about it.
				\end{compactitem}
			\item[19 June 1853] You will find that there will be cats, and kittens leaping out of the bag continualy. ``What can come next I wonder''!! I do not know; but this I know, the Lord Allmighty will not suffer the Saints, \sout{or}neither the world to slumber upon their oars; \uline{The} \uline{time} is \uline{passed for them to fold their hands and say, ``A little more sleep, A little more slumber, A little more folding of the hands}.'' \uuline{This} \uuline{peop}le \uuline{will} \uuline{never} \uuline{see} \uuline{that} \uuline{day}, for the Lord will keep them on the alert all the time,\sout{.} they will continualy have something to contend with to keep them from droping to sleep, and it is no matter to me as to \sout{the} what means he shall use to do it.
				\begin{compactitem}
					\item I transcribed this quote myself, on the same date and from the same source as the quote above that begins ``I will now say...'' This one is on page 9, PDF page 10.
				\end{compactitem}
			\item[7 April 1875] They put me in mind of what I heard Brother Kimball say, some years ago, at the time the revelation on celestial marriage was published. Brother Kimball got to talking upon celestial marriage, and he made a comparison; said he---``The cat is out of the bag; and that is not all---this cat is going to have kittens; and that is not all, those kittens are going to have cats.''
				\begin{compactitem}
					\item I transcribed this quotation myself, on 3/7/2024, from the Deseret News account. It's in the 21 April 1875 edition, on page 6, Volume 24 number 12, overall page 182. I haven't yet been able to find any more original source than this.
				\end{compactitem}
		\end{compactdesc}
