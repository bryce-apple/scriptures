				\begin{compactdesc}
					\item[BDAG 228b] \textbf{}
						\begin{compactitem}
							\item[1] \textbf{to influence one's opinion about another so as to enhance the latter's reputation, \textit{praise, honor, extol}}
								\begin{compactitem}
									\item In discussion of this entry, they gloss \hyperref[ROMANS-11-13]{Romans 11:13} as \textit{I take pride in my ministry} or \textit{I take my assignment seriously}.
								\end{compactitem}
							\item[2] \textbf{to cause to have splendid greatness, \textit{clothe in splendor, glorify}}, of the glory that comes in the next life...it is a favorite term in John..., in which the whole life of Jesus is depicted as a glorifying of the Son by the Father: \hyperref[JOHN-8-54]{John 8:54}; \hyperref[JOHN-12-18]{12:28}; \hyperref[JOHN-13-31]{13:31}; \hyperref[JOHN-17-1]{17:1}, \hyperref[JOHN-17-4]{4}...and, at the same time, of the Father by the Son: \hyperref[JOHN-13-31]{13:31f}; \hyperref[JOHN-14-13]{14:13}; \hyperref[JOHN-17-1]{17:1}. The glorifying of the Son is brought about by the miracles which the Father has him perform (\hyperref[JOHN-11-4]{11:4})...through the working of the \hyperref[PARAKLETOS]{Paraclete} (\hyperref[JOHN-16-14]{16:14}) and through `his own' (\hyperref[JOHN-17-10]{ 17:10}), who also glorify the Father (\hyperref[JOHN-15-8]{15:8}), esp. in martyrdom (\hyperref[JOHN-21-19]{21:19}).
								\begin{compactitem}
									\item This entry later glosses \hyperref[HEBREWS-5-5]{Hebrews 5:5} as \textit{he did not presume for himself the prestige of the high priesthood}.
								\end{compactitem}

	\subsubsection{Book of Mormon} % *BOM
	% -----
		\label{MAGNIFY-BOM}
	
		\begin{tabular}{ll}
			Total occurrences in the BOM & 5
		\end{tabular}
		
		Of these five uses, one has the reflexive object of a ``saw'' (2 Nephi 20:15; see \hyperref[ISAIAH-10-15]{Isaiah 10:15}); one has an object of a ``name'' (2 Nephi 25:13); two have ``office'' (the Jacob uses); and one has ``these things which ye shall write'' (Ether).
