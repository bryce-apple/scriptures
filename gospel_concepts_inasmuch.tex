\subsection{1828 American Dictionary of the English Language} % *1828
% ----------------------
	\label{INASMUCH-1828}

	(This definition is for ``insomuch,'' and is not as helpful as you would want.)

	\begin{compactenum}
		\item So that; to that degree.
	\end{compactenum}

\subsection{Oxford English Dictionary} % *OED
% ----------------------
	\label{INASMUCH-OED}

	For ``inasmuch as'':

	\begin{compactitem}
		\item[I.1] In so far as, to such a degree as, in proportion as, according as.
		\item[I.2] In that; in view of the fact that; seeing that; considering that; on the ground or for the reason that; since, because.
	\end{compactitem}
