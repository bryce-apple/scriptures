	% -----
	\subsubsection{Historical Background}
	% -----
		\label{DC40-HB}
		
		% --
		\paragraph{JSP}
		% --
		
			``By 6 January 1831, the day after a revelation called him to preach the gospel,%
			\footnote{%
				% \label{}%
				\hyperref[DC39]{DC 39}.
				}
			Methodist minister James Covel had departed Fayette, New York, leaving JS and Sidney Rigdon searching for an explanation. As JS later recalled, after Covel `rejected the word of the Lord, and returned to his former principles and people,' the Lord gave JS and Sidney Rigdon this 6 January 1831 revelation, `explaining why he obeyed not the word.'''

	\subsubsection{Section 40:1} % *DC40-1 
	% -----
		\label{DC40-1}

		BEHOLD, verily I say unto you, that the heart of my servant James Covill was right before me, for he \hyperref[COVENANT]{covenanted}%
			\footnote{%
				% \label{}%
				Notice that the Lord uses the word ``covenant'' here to describe the promise that Covill made with him. There wouldn't have been a formal priesthood ordinance or anything like that, just an agreement.
				}
		with me that he would obey my word.
