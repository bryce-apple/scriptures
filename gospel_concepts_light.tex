	\subsubsection{The ``light of Christ'' (and ``spirit of Christ'')}
	% -----
		\label{LIGHT-christ}
		
		Some points:
		
			\begin{compactitem}
				\item The ``light of Christ'' is somewhat equated with the ``Spirit of Christ'' in \hyperref[MORONI-7-16]{Moroni 7:16} and \hyperref[MORONI-7-18]{Moroni 7:18--19}. Note especially that verse 16 says that we get the spirit of Christ that we ``may know good from evil,'' and verse 19 says we should ``search diligently in the light of Christ that [we] may know good from evil.''
					\begin{compactitem}
						\item See the \hyperref[SPIRIT-PHRASES]{occurrences} of ``Spirit of Christ.''
					\end{compactitem}
				\item See \hyperref[DC29-30]{DC 29:30} and \hyperref[DC29-31]{31} and notes there, and \hyperref[DC88-7]{DC 88:7--13}.
					\begin{compactitem}
						\item More evidence, though somewhat indirect, that the ``light of Christ'' and the ``spirit of Christ'' are the same thing---again just different terms used to call attention to different aspects of its function or different ways we experience it.
						\item It is some kind of power or force---note places like \hyperref[2NEPHI-2-8]{2 Nephi 2:8} which connect this with particular things Christ did---see also \hyperref[DC33-16]{DC 33:16}, \hyperref[DC76-12]{DC 76:12}, and \hyperref[DC76-116]{DC 76:116} and \hyperref[DC76-118]{118}.
					\end{compactitem}
				\item See the ``law of the spirit of life'' at \hyperref[ROMANS-8-2]{Romans 8:2}? Reminds me of the beginning of \hyperref[DC88]{DC 88}.
			\end{compactitem}
