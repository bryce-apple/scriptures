		\label{SACRAMENT-KEYS}
	
		\begin{compactitem}
			\item The story of the \hyperref[LCHRIST-064-J]{woman at the well} includes discussion of both water and solid food.
			\item \hyperref[DC27-2]{DC 27:2} has several important points about how we perform the sacrament, how it has an effect, and what we are supposed to understand with the bread and the water (or whatever food or drink we use to do the ordinance).
		\end{compactitem}
