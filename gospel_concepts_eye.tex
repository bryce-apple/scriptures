\subsection{Key Phrases} % *KEYPHRASES *PHRASES
% ----------------------
	\label{EYE-PHRASES}

	\begin{compactdesc}
	
		\item[eye to eye] \textbf{8} | \hyperref[ISAIAH-52-8]{Isaiah 52:8}; \hyperref[MOSIAH-12-22]{Mosiah 12:22}; \hyperref[MOSIAH-15-29]{Mosiah 15:29}; \hyperref[MOSIAH-16-1]{Mosiah 16:1}; \hyperref[ALMA-36-26]{Alma 36:26}; \hyperref[3NEPHI-16-18]{3 Nephi 16:18}; \hyperref[3NEPHI-20-32]{3 Nephi 20:32}; \hyperref[DC84-98]{DC 84:98}
			\begin{compactdesc}
				\item[Bible anchor verses] 
					\begin{compactdesc}
						\item[{\hyperref[ISAIAH-52-8]{Isaiah 52:8}}] \he{`ayiN b*:`ayiN}
					\end{compactdesc}
				\item[Commentary] See the phrase ``see eye to eye'' at \hyperref[SEE-PHRASES]{See}.
				\item AB 19A, page 342, says, ``the fact that the watchmen witness with their own eyes, literally `eye to eye' (\he{`ayiN b*:`ayiN}) is consistent with this reading of the prophet as the watchman since `eye to eye' is a type of expression (cf. `face to face,' `mouth to mouth') denoting an especially close relationship with the deity (\hyperref[NUMBERS-14-14]{Num 14:14}).''
				\item DCH PDF 3514, meaning \#1.a.10 for \he{`ayiN}, says, `` `eye to eye' contact between humans (\hyperref[JEREMIAH-32-4]{Jr 32:4} \hyperref[JEREMIAH-34-3]{34:3}), Y. appears to humans `eye to eye', i.e. face to face (\hyperref[NUMBERS-14-14]{Nm 14:14} 4QDibHam-a 3.2:7; cf. Is 52:8).''
				\item That Qumran quote is, ``[... eye] to eye have you been seen in our midst,'' where I think the ``you'' is the Lord (see DSSSE PDF 1036). Note also that on the next line, 3.2:8, we read, ``[...] and we have heard your holy words'' (the ``[...]'' represent lacuna of unspecified lengths). It makes me think of the language about eyes and ears in the \hyperref[TEMPLE-ENDOWMENT-INIT-PRE-2005-WASH]{initiatory}.
				\item The Qumran text is the same in Hebrew as in Isaiah 52:8. The first \he{`yN} is reconstructed, but then it's \he{b`yN}, just like in Isaiah.
			\end{compactdesc}
