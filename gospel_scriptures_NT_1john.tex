I think this is the only use of \hyperref[NIKE]{\grl{νίκη}} in the NT, and here it refers to the means for winning the victory. So a better translation might be something like, ``This is the force for victory, the force that overcomes the world: faith.'' \gr{πίστις} is nominative.
					}
