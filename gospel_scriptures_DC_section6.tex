	\subsubsection{Note on the JSP and BC versions}
	% -----
	
		The version featured in the JSP comes from BC, so for this section they are the same; the BC is therefore not included in the alternate versions below.
	
	% -----
	\subsubsection{Historical Background}
	% -----
		\label{DC6-HB}
		
		% --
		\paragraph{JSP}
		% --
		
			``This revelation was dictated for Oliver Cowdery soon after he arrived in Harmony, Pennsylvania, and began serving as JS's scribe for the translation of the plates. JS's history stated, `Two days after the arrival of M\supers{r} Cowdery (being the seventh of April) I commenced to translate the book of Mormon and he commenced to write for me, which having continued for some time I enquired of the Lord through the Urim and Thummin and obtained the following revelation.' The exact date this text was dictated is unknown. The revelation apparently answered questions Cowdery had contemplated but not expressed to JS. According to Cowdery's friend David Whitmer, `Soon after Oliver's arrival in Harmony, he wrote to me and said that Joseph had envuired [inquired] of the Lord concerning him, and had told him secrets of his life that he knew could not be known to any person but himself, in any other way than by revelation from the Almighty.'%
				\footnote{%
					% \label{}%
					The JSP note here says, ``James H. Hart, `About the Book of Mormon,' \textit{Deseret News} (Salt Lake City), 9 Apr. 1884, 190; see also `Report of Elders Orson Pratt and Joseph F. Smith,' \textit{Deseret News}, 27 Nov. 1878, 674.''
					}
			
			``JS's history recounted, `After we had received this revelation he (Oliver Cowdery) stated to me that after he had gone to my father's to board, and after the family communicated to him concerning my having got the plates, that one night after he had retired to bed, he called upon the Lord to know if these things were so, and that the Lord had manifested to him that they were true.' JS also explained that Cowdery had seen the Lord and the plates in a vision and `was desiorous to come and write' for JS in Harmony. The revelation featured below built on that experience by announcing that if Cowdery desired, the Lord would grant him a gift `to translate even as my servant Joseph.'''

		Seek not for riches but for \hyperref[WISDOM]{wisdom},%
			\footnote{%
				% \label{}%
				Notice that we are specifically instructed to seek for wisdom here. Given the warnings about certain kinds of wisdom elsewhere (\hyperref[1CORINTHIANS-3-19]{1 Corinthians 3:19}, \hyperref[MOSIAH-24-7]{Mosiah 24:7}), the Lord must have some particular ``wisdom'' in mind that he is instructing us to seek (compare \hyperref[1CORINTHIANS-2-6]{1 Corinthians 2:6--7}, \hyperref[ALMA-38-9]{Alma 38:9}, \hyperref[DC10-34]{DC 10:34}). In the DC, the Lord says several times something like ``Here is wisdom,'' and then gives a bit of instruction---so maybe part of the wisdom we are seeking is guidance from God through the Holy Ghost about particular matters. I think that taking ``wisdom,'' at least in part, as ``that which God reveals to us about something'' is a good start for making sense of what we are being instructed to seek; we can also think of this instruction along the lines of \hyperref[JACOB-4-8]{Jacob 4:8}.
				}
		and behold, the \hyperref[MYSTERY]{mysteries} of God shall be \hyperref[UNFOLD]{unfolded} unto you, and then shall you be made rich. Behold, he that hath eternal life is rich.
