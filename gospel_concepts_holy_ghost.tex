	\subsubsection{Identity and character of the Holy Ghost}
	% -----
		\label{HOLYGHOST-identity}
		
		Here are some thoughts I've gathered:
		
		\begin{compactitem}
			\item The Holy Ghost is sometimes perceived in the form of a person \hyperref[1NEPHI-11-11]{1 Nephi 11:11}.
		\end{compactitem} 
		
	% -----
	\subsubsection{Functions of the Holy Ghost}
	% -----
		\label{HOLYGHOST-functions}
		
		\begin{compactitem}
			\item Enlighten our minds (\hyperref[DC11-13]{DC 11:13})
			\item Fill our souls with joy (\hyperref[DC11-13]{DC 11:13})
			\item Entice us (\hyperref[MOSIAH-3-19]{Mosiah 3:19})
			\item The spirit of freedom (\hyperref[ALMA-61-15]{Alma 61:15})
			\item Strive with us (\hyperref[1NEPHI-7-14]{1 Nephi 7:14})
				\begin{compactitem}
					\item I find this one and the ``entice'' one really interesting, since it speaks to the very different ways that the Holy Ghost is trying to work with us in order to get us where we need to go.
					\item See also \hyperref[2NEPHI-26-11]{2 Nephi 26:11}, 
				\end{compactitem}
			\item Constrain us (\hyperref[1NEPHI-7-15]{1 Nephi 7:15})
				\begin{compactitem}
					\item See also \hyperref[2NEPHI-28-1]{2 Nephi 28:1}
				\end{compactitem}
			\item Bear testimony of Christ (\hyperref[1NEPHI-12-18]{1 Nephi 12:18})
		\end{compactitem}
