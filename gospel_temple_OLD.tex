			\item[CDBY 28--29] Let them do what God requires. Now, query---could Moses have obtained the law if he had stayed in the midst of the children of Israel, instead of going up on the mountain? Our endowments and sealing powers and many blessings depend upon it...

	\subsection{Clothing} % *TEMPLECLOTHING
	
		\begin{description}
		\item[Swedenborg] In Swedenborg's own copy of \textit{Vera Christiana Religio}, on the back cover, he writes a list of gifts that he received in the spirit world. Some of these are items of clothing and other things, which are interesting in the light of Mormonism: ``A handsome red chest with five compartments and five drawers in each compartment. A handsome suit of clothes; a fine cap. A little crown containing five small diamonds, which is worn in heaven on one side of the head. A beautiful little rose, with a most brilliant diamond in the center, which was afterwards set in a gold ring. A ‘caraph' or decoration to be worn on the head. A necklace of diamonds. A pendant of gold containing a diamond. A bracelet of diamonds. Earrings, each containing three diamonds. A capsule in a casket containing shining crystals, by which is signified regeneration to eternity. Something precious for the hand, which was placed in a beautiful case on November 28, 1770. A pendant in the form of a jewel, containing a beautiful diamond. A fine hat for me. Something precious which cannot be seen by spirits, only by angels, on May 28, 1771. A cane with a fine gold-top, on August 13, 1771.''
		\end{description}
