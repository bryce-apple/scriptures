	\subsubsection{The disjunctive vs. explanatory ``or''}
	% ----- 
		\label{OR-COMMENTARY-explanatory}
	
		In the BOM and possibly in other scriptures of the restoration, the word ``or'' is used with at least two distinct and important meanings:
		
		\begin{enumerate}
			\item A \textit{disjunctive} meaning, usually with \textit{exclusive} force, to indicate one or two possibilities or options.
			\item An \textit{explanatory} meaning, where ``or'' is short for ``or, in other words,'' and glosses or further explains what was just said.
		\end{enumerate}
		
		I am most interested in uses of the explanatory meaning.
