		\label{MALACHI-4}
		
		Some of the verses in this chapter appear in various places in restoration scripture and elsewhere; for comparisons, see \hyperref[EXSS-OT-MALACHI-4-VERSIONS]{this study}.
		
		% % ---
		\paragraph{Malachi 4:1} % *MALACHI-4-1
		% % ---
			\label{MALACHI-4-1}

			For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble: and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the LORD of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch.

			\begin{versebox}
					\item[BHS] \he{כִּֽי־הִנֵּ֤ה הַיּוֹם֙ בָּ֔א בֹּעֵ֖ר כַּתַּנּ֑וּר וְהָי֨וּ כָל־זֵדִ֜ים וְכָל־עֹשֵׂ֤ה רִשְׁעָה֙ קַ֔שׁ וְלִהַ֨ט אֹתָ֜ם הַיּ֣וֹם הַבָּ֗א אָמַר֙ יְהוָ֣ה צְבָא֔וֹת אֲשֶׁ֛ר לֹא־יַעֲזֹ֥ב לָהֶ֖ם שֹׁ֥רֶשׁ וְעָנָֽף׃}
					% % \item[LXX] \gr{}
					% % \item[BSV]
					% % \item[AB ]
					% % \item[NETS]
					% % \item[OTR1]
					% % \item[OTR2]
					\item[ASV] For, behold, the day cometh, it burneth as a furnace; and all the proud, and all that work wickedness, shall be stubble; and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith Jehovah of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch.
					\item[NIV] “Surely the day is coming; it will burn like a furnace. All the arrogant and every evildoer will be stubble, and the day that is coming will set them on fire,” says the LORD Almighty. “Not a root or a branch will be left to them.
					\item[NRSV] See, the day is coming, burning like an oven, when all the arrogant and all evildoers will be stubble; the day that comes shall burn them up, says the LORD of hosts, so that it will leave them neither root nor branch.
