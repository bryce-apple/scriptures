	\subsubsection{Seeing as the basic epistemic state in the gospel}
	% -----
		\label{SEE-epistemic}
		
			\begin{compactitem}
				\item See \hyperref[JACOB-7-12]{Jacob 7:12}; notice how these ways of knowing are treated separately
				\item See uses of ``surety'' in the scriptures, and how many of them relate to empirical and in particular visual witnesses
				\item \hyperref[JOHN-20-8]{John 20:8}
				\item Find uses of ``see'' and forms of ``know'' in the same area, like:
					\begin{compactitem}
						\item \hyperref[DC50-45]{DC 50:45}
						\item \hyperref[DC76-117]{DC 76:117}
						\item \hyperref[DC84-119]{DC 84:119}
						\item \hyperref[DC93-1]{DC 93:1}
					\end{compactitem}
				\item \hyperref[MOSIAH-27-22]{Mosiah 27:22} 
					\begin{compactitem}
						\item See also verses 32 and 35 of this chapter.
					\end{compactitem}
				\item \hyperref[ALMA-36-26]{Alma 36:26}
					\begin{compactitem}
						\item Maybe this is the culminatory ``seeing'' experience that finishes off the perfect knowledge from Alma 32:21? This verse clearly goes with that chapter.
					\end{compactitem}
				\item \hyperref[ETHER-12-5]{Ether 12:5}, very important about seeing and belief; I think this chapter has to be read in conjunction with Alma 32.
					\begin{compactitem}
						\item Also verses 19--21 in this same chapter.
					\end{compactitem}
			\end{compactitem}
