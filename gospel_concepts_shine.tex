							\item[1a] as active...in our literature only intransitive \textit{shine, give light, be bright}
							\item[1b] passive, in active sense, of light and its sources \textit{shine, flash}
							\item[2] to become visible, \textit{appear}
							\item[2a] \textit{appear, be} or \textit{become visible, be revealed}
							\item[2b] \textit{make one's appearance, show oneself}
							\item[3] to become known, \textit{be recognized, be apparent, be revealed}
							\item[3a] with predicate nominative
							\item[3b] \textit{appear} to the eyes of the spirit, \textit{be revealed}
							\item[4] to be known by appearance as opposed to underlying reality, \textit{appear as something, appear to be something}
