\subsection{1828 American Dictionary of the English Language} % *1828
% ----------------------
	\label{RIGHT-NOUN-1828}

	\begin{compactitem}
		\item[1] Conformity to the will of God, or to his law, the perfect standard of truth and justice.
		\item[2] Conformity to human laws, or to other human standard of truth, propriety or justice.
		\item[3] Justice; that which is due or proper.
		\item[4] Freedom from error; conformity with truth or fact.
		\item[5] Just claim; legal title; ownership; the legal power of exclusive possession and enjoyment.
		\item[6] Just claim by courtesy, customs, or the principles of civility and decorum.
		\item[7] Just claim by sovereignty; prerogative. 
		\item[8] That which justly belongs to one.
		\item[9] Property; interest.
		\item[10] Just claim; immunity; privilege. All men have a \textit{right} to the secure enjoyment of life, personal safety, liberty and property. We deem the \textit{right} of trial by jury invaluable, particularly in the case of crimes. Rights are natural, civil, political, religious, personal, and public.
		\item[11] Authority; legal power.
		\item[12] In the United States, a tract of land; or a share or proportion of property, as in a mine or manufactory.
		\item[13] The side opposite to the left.
	\end{compactitem}
