		% --
		\paragraph{HDDC}
		% --
		
			``Zera Pulsipher was one of those that may have accompanied the Prophet on this occasion. Following is his description of the area:
			
			\begin{quote}
				``Davies Co was a buetiful place situated on grand river the first rate land and plenty of good timber where we supposed there had been an ancient citty of the Nephites as the hewn stone were already there in piles also the Mound or ater built by father where to offer sacrifices when he was old leaning upon his staff%
					\footnote{%
						% \label{}%
						See \hyperref[DC107-56]{DC 107:56}.
						}
				Prophecying the Most Noted thing that should take place down to the latest generation therefore it was calld Adam ondiamen''%
					\footnote{%
						% \label{}%
						The HDDC footnote says that Zerah (``Zera'' is wrong I think) Pulsipher's journal is in the church archives but I haven't been able to find a copy of it.
						}
			\end{quote}

			\footnote{%
				% \label{}%
				See the ``\hyperref[EMS-1832-JS-CIR-4-MAR-1832-LANG]{sample of pure language}'' from early 1832. This term first appears at \hyperref[DC78-15]{DC 78:15}; see also \hyperref[DC107-53]{DC 107:53}.
