Young occupied some time instructing the Saints in the principles of government, showing that men wielding authority and power can rule so tyranically that the feelings, affections and love of those whom they govern will be turned from them. Parents who rule their children with iron severity estrange those children from them, when an opposite course would draw them closer and bind their affections to them. So in the various organizations in the kingdom of God. The priesthood is the power of God; it rules in the eternal world, and should be recognized by all the inhabitants of the earth.
