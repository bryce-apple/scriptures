					% \label{}%
					This verse is the biblical anchor for the phrase ``sensual and devilish,'' which occurs here and five times in restoration scripture: \hyperref[MOSIAH-16-3]{Mosiah 16:3}, \hyperref[ALMA-42-10]{Alma 42:10}, \hyperref[DC20-20]{DC 20:20}, \hyperref[MOSES-5-13]{Moses 5:13}, and \hyperref[MOSES-6-49]{Moses 6:49}. Of these six occurrences, four also have ``carnal''---the two lacking it are the one here in James, and \hyperref[DC20-20]{DC 20:20}. In the NT both \gr{σάρκινος} and \gr{σαρκικός} are used there. ``Sensual'' is \gr{ψυχική} here, and the \hyperref[PSUCHIKOS]{BDAG entry} mentions this verse for meaning \#1.a. Note that it's the same word that occurs in the ``natural man'' construction of \hyperref[1CORINTHIANS-2-14]{1 Corinthians 2:14}.
