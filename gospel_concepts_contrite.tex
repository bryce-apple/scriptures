\subsection{1828 American Dictionary of the English Language} % *1828
% ----------------------
	\label{CONTRITE-1828}

	\begin{compactitem}
		\item Literally, worn or bruised. Hence, broken-hearted for sin; deeply affected with grief and sorrow for having offended God; humble; penitent; as a \textit{contrite} sinner.
	\end{compactitem}

% ----------------------
\subsection{Oxford English Dictionary} % *OED
% ----------------------
	\label{CONTRITE-OED}
	
	Etymology: ``< French \textit{contrit} (12th cent.), < Latin \textit{contrītus} bruised, crushed, past participle of \textit{conterĕre}, < \textit{con}- together + \textit{terĕre} to rub, triturate, bray, grind.''

	\begin{compactitem}
		\item[1] \textit{literal.} Bruised, crushed; worn or broken by rubbing. \textit{Obsolete. rare.}
		\item[2.a] \textit{figurative.} Crushed or broken in spirit by a sense of sin, and so brought to complete penitence.
		\item[2.b] Of actions, etc.: Displaying, or arising from, contrition.
	\end{compactitem}

\subsection{Buck's Theological Dictionary (1833)} % *BTD
% ----------------------
	\label{CONTRITE-BTD}

	\begin{compactdesc}
		\item[250] This word signifies beaten or bruised, as with hard blows, or a heavy burden; and so in Scripture language imports one whose heart is broken and wounded for sin, in opposition to the heart of stone. \hyperref[ISAIAH-66-2]{Is. lxvi. 2.} \hyperref[PSALMS-51-17]{Ps. li. 17.} Ps. lvii. 15. \textit{[This reference says Psalms but it must be a mistake and should refer to \hyperref[ISAIAH-57-15]{Isaiah 57:15}.]} The evidences of a broken and contrite spirit are,
			\begin{compactitem}
				\item Deep conviction of the evil of sin.---
				\item Humiliation under a sense of it. Job xliii. 5, 6.---
					\begin{compactitem}
						\item There is no Job 43:5--6; maybe \hyperref[JOB-42-6]{Job 42:6}?
					\end{compactitem}
				\item Pungent sorrow for it. \hyperref[ZECHARIAH-12-10]{Zec. xii. 10.}---
				\item Ingenuous confession of it. \hyperref[1JOHN-1-9]{1 John i. 9.}---
				\item Prayer for deliverance from it. \hyperref[PSALMS-51-10]{Ps. li. 10.} \hyperref[LUKE-18-13]{Luke xviii. 13.}---
				\item Susceptibility of good impressions. \hyperref[EZEKIEL-11-19]{Ezek. xi. 19.}
			\end{compactitem}
	\end{compactdesc}
