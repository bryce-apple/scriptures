	\subsubsection{Key Points}
	% -----
		\label{PRAYER-KEYS}
	
		\begin{compactitem}
			\item We pray in the name of Jesus Christ because Christ is the source through which we receive the power that ultimately derives from the Father. So when we pray in Christ's name, we are following the chain back up to the ultimate source, through the source from which it comes to us.
		\end{compactitem}
