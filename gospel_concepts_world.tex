			%
				\label{KOSMOS}
			
				\begin{compactdesc}
					\item[BDAG 497a] \textbf{}
						\begin{compactitem}
							\item[1] that which serves to beautify through decoration, \textit{adornment, adorning}
							\item[2] condition of orderliness, \textit{orderly arrangement, order}
							\item[3] the sum total of everything here and now, \textit{the world, the (orderly) universe}
							\item[4] the sum total of all beings above the level of the animals, \textit{the world}
							\item[5] planet earth as a place of inhabitation, \textit{the world}
							\item[5a] generally \textit{(Matthew 4:8 is listed here.)}
							\item[5b] \textit{the world} as the habitation of humanity
							\item[5c] \textit{earth, world} in contrast to heaven
							\item[5d] \textit{the world} outside in contrast to one's home
							\item[6] humanity in general, \textit{the world}
							\item[6a] generally
							\item[6b] of all humanity, but especially of believers, as the object of God's love
							\item[7] the system of human existence in its many aspects, \textit{the world}
							\item[7a] as scene of earthly joys, possessions, cares, sufferings
							\item[7b] \textit{the world}, and everything that belongs to it, appears as that which is hostile to God, i.e. lost in sin, wholly at odds with anything divine, ruined and depraved
							\item[8] collective aspect of an entity, \textit{totality, sum total}
