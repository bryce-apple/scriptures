	% -----
	\subsubsection{Key Points}
	% -----
		\label{BAPTISM-KEYS}
	
		\begin{compactitem}
			\item The manner of baptism---of submerging someone in the water and then pulling them out---is ``after the manner of [Christ's] burial,'' and we are ``buried in the water in his name'' (\hyperref[DC76-51]{DC 76:51}).
			\item \hyperref[DC35-5]{DC 35:5--6}---Rigdon was baptizing ``by water unto repentance,'' but his followers were not receiving ``the Holy Ghost.''
		\end{compactitem}

	\subsubsection{Why do we need to be baptized?}
	% -----
		\label{BAPTISM-necessity}
		
		To start, one answer is that we need to initialize a law-bound relationship with Christ---we need to hook ourselves into the part of the system of priesthood laws that has to do with salvation, and we do it by being baptized.
